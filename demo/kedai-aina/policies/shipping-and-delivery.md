# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times and fees

| Destination | Delivery time | Fee | Free delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Klang Valley | 1 to 2 working days | RM 8 | Orders above RM 150 |
| Rest of Peninsular Malaysia | 2 to 4 working days | RM 12 | Orders above RM 200 |
| Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan | 5 to 9 working days | RM 25 | Orders above RM 500 |

## Same-day delivery

Klang Valley orders paid before 11am on a working day can be delivered the same day for RM 20. Same-day delivery is not available for vacuums or air purifiers.

## Tracking

A tracking number is sent by email and WhatsApp once an order ships. Orders in Peninsular Malaysia are delivered by J&T Express, and orders to Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan by Pos Laju.
