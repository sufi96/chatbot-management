# Warranty and Returns Policy

## Warranty periods

| Product line | Warranty | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Air fryers (X200, X100) | 24 months | Heating element, fan and control panel |
| Microwave ovens (M8) | 24 months | Magnetron and control panel |
| Cordless vacuums (V9) | 24 months | Motor; the battery is covered for 12 months |
| Air purifiers (A6) | 36 months | Motor and sensors; filters are not covered |
| Kettles, rice cookers, blenders, irons and fans | 12 months | Manufacturing defects |

A warranty claim needs the order number or the receipt. Damage from misuse, power surges or repairs by anyone other than Kedai Aina is not covered.

## Returns

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery, and only if they are faulty.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 working days. Shipping fees are refunded only when the item was faulty or the wrong item was sent.
